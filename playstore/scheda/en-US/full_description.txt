Scontrini Trasferta collects your business-trip receipts and prepares the expense report to send, with no account and no server: your data stays on your phone. The app is in Italian.

JUST TAKE A PHOTO
• Snap the receipt: the app reads the amount, the tip and the time, even from a tilted photo.
• Tap the right number directly on the photo if the reading picked the wrong one.
• 10, 15 or 20% tip in one tap, even when the receipt doesn't show it.
• Location tells restaurant, hotel or gas station apart and suggests the category.

NO CHARGE FORGOTTEN
• Card charges wait in "Da completare" until they have a receipt: from shared bank SMS, from an automation such as MacroDroid or from the bank statement (CSV, Excel or PDF), without duplicates.
• Evening reminder for charges still without a photo.
• Lost receipt? Create the substitute declaration as a PDF.

ALLOWANCES AND INFLATION
• Meal and hotel allowance check by country, with the bill split among diners.
• Every meal and hotel PDF includes a page with the country's inflation: official World Bank data, IMF forecast estimate and the allowance revalued to the year of the expense.
• ECB exchange rate of each expense's date and total in euro.

READY TO SEND
• Pre-submission check: expenses without photo, taxis without route, charges without receipt.
• One PDF per expense under 600 KB, a single PDF for bulk upload, expense lines with separate tips and a CSV summary, numbered in bank-transaction order.
• Everything in a ZIP folder to send to OneDrive or by email.

FOR TEAMS ON THE ROAD
• "Sono a tavola": colleagues join as diners via QR code, NFC or Bluetooth.
• History split by trip, with dates and totals per currency.
• Optional shared repository to use the app on several phones, plus file backup.

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no account. Data is encrypted on the phone and the app can be locked with fingerprint or PIN. It leaves the phone only when you share it.

The allowance zone and the meal allowance can be set in Settings to match your company policy. The app is not affiliated with any company or expense-management software.
